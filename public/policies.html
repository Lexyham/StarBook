StarBook ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it when you use our platform at starbook.com.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Account registration: Full name, email address, and password (stored as bcrypt hash - never in plain text).
  • Booking data: Recipient name, event type, event date, and booking instructions you provide.
  • Payment proof: Screenshots or images of transaction confirmations you upload. We do not store payment card numbers.
  • Gift card data: Card brand and redemption code entered at checkout. Codes are deleted after verification.
  • Profile information: Optional phone number, country, and profile photo you choose to add.
  • Support messages: Name, email, and message content when you contact our support team.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, and time spent on our platform.
  • Device data: Browser type, operating system, screen resolution, and IP address.
  • Cookies and local storage: Authentication tokens stored in localStorage to keep you signed in. See our Cookie Policy for details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and manage your celebrity bookings and fan card orders.
  • To verify payment and confirm transactions with our admin team.
  • To send booking confirmations, status updates, and important account notifications via email (Brevo).
  • To personalise your dashboard experience and show relevant celebrities.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To detect and prevent fraud and unauthorized account access.
  • To improve our platform based on aggregate usage patterns.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Celebrities and their representatives: Your first name and event details are shared with the celebrity once your booking is approved.
  • Brevo (email service): Your email address and name are used solely to send transactional emails.
  • Hosting providers: Our infrastructure provider may process data as part of server operations. All providers are contractually bound to data protection obligations.
  • Legal authorities: We may disclose data if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption for all data in transit, bcrypt password hashing, JWT-based stateless authentication, and server-side input validation. However, no system is 100% secure - we encourage you to use a strong, unique password.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Update inaccurate or incomplete data via your dashboard Profile tab.
  • Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Restriction: Request we restrict processing while a dispute is resolved.

To exercise any right, contact us at privacy@starbook.com. We respond within 30 days.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Booking records are retained for 3 years for legal and dispute-resolution purposes. Support messages are deleted after 12 months. You may request earlier deletion at any time.

This Cookie Policy explains how StarBook uses cookies and similar storage technologies on our platform.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser. We also use browser localStorage and sessionStorage for similar purposes. These help us remember your preferences and keep you signed in.

What We Store and Why

Essential (Required)

  • sb-token (localStorage): Your JWT authentication token. Required to keep you signed in. Expires when you log out or after 7 days. Cannot be disabled without breaking the sign-in functionality.
  • sb-user (localStorage): Your cached user profile (name, email, account type). Used to display your name in the navbar without an extra API call. Cleared on logout.

Analytics (Optional)

We may use anonymised analytics to understand how users interact with the platform (page views, feature usage). No personally identifiable information is included in analytics data. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

Managing Cookies

You can clear localStorage and sessionStorage at any time through your browser's developer tools or privacy settings. Clearing sb-token will sign you out. You can also use your browser settings to block all cookies; however, this will prevent you from signing in.

Third-Party Storage

We load Google Fonts (font files only - no tracking cookies set by Google Fonts when using font files directly). No other third-party cookies or trackers are used on our platform.

Changes to This Policy

We will update this page when our storage practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects when this policy was last revised.

Questions about cookies: privacy@starbook.com
