Payment Guides

Paying with Cryptocurrency

We accept BTC, ETH, USDT (TRC20), BNB, and XRP. Follow these steps exactly.

1
Choose your coin

Select your preferred cryptocurrency on the payment step. We recommend USDT (TRC20) for the fastest confirmation times and lowest fees.

2
Copy the wallet address

Click the "Copy" button next to the wallet address. Never type wallet addresses by hand - one wrong character and funds are lost permanently.

3
Send the exact amount

Open your crypto wallet app and send the exact amount shown. Do not subtract any fees - ensure the full amount arrives on our end.

4
Upload payment proof

Take a screenshot of your transaction confirmation (showing TX hash, amount, and destination address) and upload it on the booking form.

5
Wait for confirmation

Bitcoin takes 1–3 confirmations (~30 min), ETH and USDT take 1 confirmation (~5 min), BNB takes under 2 minutes. You'll receive an email once approved.

Paying with Gift Cards

We accept Razer Gold, iTunes, Google Play, Steam, and Amazon gift cards.

1
Buy a gift card

Purchase a gift card from an authorized retailer (Walmart, Best Buy, GameStop, Amazon, or the card's official app/website). Physical or digital codes both work.

2
Select card brand

On the payment tab, choose the Gift Card option, then select your card brand from the dropdown (Razer Gold, iTunes, Google Play, Steam, or Amazon).

3
Enter the card code

Enter the full redemption code exactly as printed on the card. Include all dashes (e.g. XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX). Do not redeem the code in the card's own app first.

4
Enter the card value

Enter the full USD face value of the card. The card must cover the full booking amount. We do not accept partial-value cards.

5
Await verification

Our team verifies gift card codes within 2–4 hours. Once verified, your booking is confirmed and you'll receive a confirmation email.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a celebrity?
Browse our celebrity roster on the Celebrities page, click on a celebrity's profile, and click "Book Now". Follow the steps in order: 1. Select event details, 2. Confirm your request and accept the Terms of Service, then 3. choose a payment method and submit. Our team reviews submitted bookings within 24–48 hours.
What payment methods do you accept?
Currently available for checkout: cryptocurrency and gift cards, subject to the configured options shown on the payment screen. Gift card and crypto submissions are reviewed manually by StarBook staff. Card checkout. Bank Transfer and CashApp are unavailable due to maintenance; Mobile Wallet services (PayPal, Paystack, and Flutterwave) are also under maintenance. Only use wallet addresses currently displayed in checkout.
What is your refund policy?
We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all approved bookings if the celebrity fails to deliver. Refund requests must be submitted via our contact form within 14 days of your booking's scheduled date. Refunds are processed back in the original payment currency within 3–5 business days. Note: pending or rejected bookings where payment proof was not verified are not eligible.
How do celebrity fan cards work?
Fan cards are personalized membership cards featuring your name, your favourite celebrity, a unique fan ID number, and a QR code. They come in three tiers - Silver ($29), Gold ($59), and VIP ($99) - each with increasing premium features. Cards ship worldwide within 5–10 business days after order approval. You can order a fan card from any celebrity's profile page or the Fan Card page.
How long does a booking take to be confirmed?
After you submit a booking with payment proof, our team reviews it within 24–48 hours on business days. Crypto payments with clear transaction screenshots are typically faster (4–8 hours). Gift card verifications take 2–4 hours. You'll receive an email notification when your booking is approved or if we need additional information.
What happens after my booking is approved?
Once approved, the celebrity will reach out via the contact method associated with your booking (typically email) within 3–5 business days to coordinate the event. For video messages and shoutouts, delivery is usually within 7 days. For live events or appearances, details will be arranged directly.
How do I create an account?
Click "Register" in the navigation bar. Enter your full name, username, email address, and a password of at least 6 characters. Optionally enter a license key to unlock Premium access. Accept the Terms of Service and click "Create Free Account". You'll be logged in immediately.
What is a license key and how do I get one?
A license key unlocks Premium features on your account, including priority celebrity access, advanced AI search, and priority support. License keys are distributed through official StarBook promotions, partnerships, and VIP packages. If you have a key, enter it during registration or contact support to apply it to an existing account.
Can I book a celebrity for a private event?
Yes. Select "Private Event" as the event type when booking. Describe the event in the instructions field. Private events (birthdays, corporate events, weddings) are reviewed case-by-case by the celebrity's team. Pricing for private appearances starts at the celebrity's listed rate; large events may be quoted separately.
Is my personal information secure?
Yes. We use JWT-based authentication, and your password is bcrypt-hashed and never stored in plain text. We do not store full payment card numbers. All API communication uses HTTPS. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.
